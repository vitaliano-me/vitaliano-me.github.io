# Privacy Policy for Android Applications

**Last Updated:** May 31, 2026

## Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how our Android applications ("App," "we," "us," or "our") collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile applications available on the Google Play Store.

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use our App. By accessing or using our App, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agree to be bound by all the provisions of this Privacy Policy.

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Information You Provide Directly

- **Account Information:** When you create an account, we may collect your name, email address, phone number, and other profile information.
- **Payment Information:** If you make purchases through the App, payment information is processed by third-party payment processors. We do not directly store credit card details.
- **User-Generated Content:** Any content you create, upload, or submit through the App.
- **Communications:** Messages, feedback, or inquiries you send to us.

### 1.2 Information Collected Automatically

- **Device Information:** Device model, operating system, unique device identifiers, and IMEI.
- **Usage Data:** Features used, actions taken, time spent on features, crash reports, and analytics data.
- **Location Data:** Precise or approximate location information (if the App requests location permissions).
- **Cookies and Similar Technologies:** We may use cookies, web beacons, and similar tracking technologies to enhance your experience.
- **Log Data:** IP addresses, access times, browser type, and pages visited.

### 1.3 Information from Third Parties

- **Social Media:** If you link your social media accounts, we collect information from those platforms.
- **Third-Party Services:** Analytics providers, advertising partners, and other service providers may share data with us.
- **Public Sources:** Information available through public databases or records.

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

- **Service Delivery:** To provide, maintain, and improve the App and its features.
- **User Accounts:** To create and manage your account, process transactions, and send transactional communications.
- **Customer Support:** To respond to your inquiries, provide support, and resolve issues.
- **Personalization:** To tailor content and features based on your preferences and usage patterns.
- **Analytics:** To understand how users interact with the App and identify trends and improvements.

- **Legal Compliance:** To comply with legal obligations, enforce agreements, and protect our rights.
- **Security:** To detect, prevent, and address fraud, security incidents, and technical issues.
- **Advertising:** To serve targeted advertisements based on your interests (with appropriate consent).

## 5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information, including:

- **Encryption:** Data transmitted over HTTPS and encrypted at rest where applicable.
- **Access Controls:** Restricted access to personal information on a need-to-know basis.
- **Firewalls and Intrusion Detection:** Technical safeguards against unauthorized access.
- **Regular Security Audits:** Periodic reviews and updates of security practices.

**No method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.** While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.
